MotoTrack

A simple Android app for motorcycle riders who work with ride apps.

MotoTrack helps you keep track of what your motorcycle really earns and costs:

  • Daily income, fuel fill-ups, oil changes, maintenance and the ride app's commission.
  • Monthly distance, fuel consumption, cost per kilometer and net profit.
  • Live trip distance measured with your phone's GPS.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive and optional sync to your own Google Sheet, using your own Google account.

Your data stays on your phone. MotoTrack has no servers and the developer cannot see your data.
