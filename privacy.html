Summary

MotoTrack stores your data on your own phone. The developer has no servers and cannot access your data. The optional Google features below store data only inside your own Google account.

Data the app stores on your phone

Income, fuel, oil, maintenance and commission entries, trip distances and durations, odometer reading, fuel price and app settings (language, theme). This data stays on your device unless you turn on one of the optional Google features.

Location

While a trip is running, the app uses your phone's location only to calculate the distance you rode. The route is not saved and the location is not sent anywhere; only the resulting distance and duration are stored on your phone.

Optional: Google sign-in and cloud backup

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It uses it to save a backup copy of the app's database in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only this app can open, and to restore it on another phone when you sign in with the same account. We do not receive this backup.

Optional: Google Sheets sync

If you choose to connect Google Sheets, the app asks for the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It uses it to create a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive and write your entries into it. The app can only access the spreadsheet it created, not your other Drive files.

Google user data

MotoTrack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and sync features described above, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking libraries, and no selling or sharing of data.

Your choices

  • Sign out inside the app to stop using the cloud backup on that phone.
  • Delete the cloud backup: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → MotoTrack → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove the app's access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the data on your phone by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
